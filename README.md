

# GreenRent 🚜

### 📌 Overview

GreenRent is a web-based platform designed to facilitate the rental of agricultural equipment in Riyadh. It connects equipment owners with renters, making the process of finding, listing, and reserving equipment easier and more efficient.

### 🎯 Objectives

* **Simplify** the process of renting agricultural equipment.


* **Help** equipment owners list and manage their equipment.


* **Allow** renters to search and reserve equipment.


* **Improve** accessibility and efficiency in the agricultural sector.



---



### ⚙️ Features

* 🔍 **Filter:** Search and filter equipment by type, price, and location.


* 📅 **Reservation:** Reserve equipment for specific dates and manage bookings.


* ➕ **Listings:** Owners can easily add, edit, or delete equipment listings.


* ⭐ **Reviews:** Trust-building through a rating and review system.


* 👨‍💼 **Admin:** Monitoring and management of users, listings, and platform policies.



---

### 🛠 Technologies Used

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript


* **Backend:** PHP


* **Database:** MySQL


* **Tools:** Jira (for project management)
